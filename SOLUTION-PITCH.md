# ScamBrake – Pause Before You Trust

### Project Overview
ScamBrake is an AI-assisted, voice-first cyber fraud detection assistant designed to help people identify suspicious messages and audio in Hindi and Indian English.

### Problem Statement
People across India increasingly encounter digital fraud attempts involving fake KYC updates, refund scams, fake customer support, phishing links, fraudulent job offers and remote-access applications. Such messages often use urgency, fear and misleading instructions to manipulate users.

### Proposed Solution
ScamBrake helps users examine suspicious communication before taking action. It analyses message text and audio transcripts, identifies potential scam indicators, highlights warning signs and provides practical safety guidance.

### Key Features
- **Hindi and English Analysis:** Supports suspicious message analysis in Hindi and Indian English.
- **Audio Transcription:** Uses Gnani AI Prisma speech-to-text integration to convert uploaded audio into text for analysis.
- **Risk Assessment:** Classifies suspicious communication into HIGH, MEDIUM and LOW risk levels.
- **Warning Sign Detection:** Highlights suspicious phrases and explains potential fraud tactics.
- **Scam Replay Lab:** Helps users understand scam scenarios and recognise manipulation techniques.
- **Situation-Specific Recovery Assistant:** Provides safety recommendations based on what the user reports happened.

### Technology Stack
- JavaScript
- Node.js and Express.js
- HTML, CSS and JavaScript
- Gnani AI Prisma speech-to-text API
- Groq AI for AI-assisted analysis and safety guidance

### Intended Impact
ScamBrake aims to make digital safety guidance easier to access, especially for users who prefer Hindi or Indian English. It encourages people to pause, recognise suspicious communication and take safer actions before sharing sensitive information or transferring money.

### Project Goal
**Pause before you trust. Think before you act. Stay safe with ScamBrake.**
